# Summary 
## Key idea:
* The strategies you use to multiply and divide rational numbers can be used to multiply and divide rational expressions. That is, if A, B, C, and D are plynomials, then
$$\frac{A}{B}* \frac{C}{D}=\frac{AC}{BD},B,C\ne 0$$
$$\frac{A}{B}\div\frac{C}{D}=\frac{A}{B}*\frac{D}{C},B,D,C \ne 0$$

* if we have a binomial, try factoring first, then try simplifying before multiplying/dividing
* identify the NPVs of the variables
* When multiplying fractions, multiply the numerator the numerator, and the denominator with the denominator
* When dividing fractions, take the reciprocal of the second fraction and then multiply
* When dividing, we must look for restrictions in 3 places, because we are going to take the reciprocal of the second fraction
* simplify using common factors

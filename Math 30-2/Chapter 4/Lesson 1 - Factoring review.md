# Vocabulary
Factoring - Factoring means to write a polynomials the product of simpler polynomials. - Factoring is a key skill for rational functions and equations.

# Greatest Common Factor 
• Always look for this first!
• Find the GCF of both terms and place it outside the brackets. 
• Divide each term inside the brackets by the GCF and write the result inside the brackets. 
• Pay attention to the signs!

# Finding GCF:
$$MATH \to NUM \to 9: gcd($$
## Example: GCF:$(x^3,x^2)=x^2$

# Example 1
## a)
$10x^2-15x$ $GCF:5x$

$\frac{10x^2-15x}{5x}$
factored: $5x(2x-3)$

## b)
$2x^4+12x$
GCF: $3x$

$\frac{3x^4+12x}{3x}$

$3x(x^3+4)$
## c)
$-16x^3-24x^2$

GCF: $8x^2$

$\frac{-16x^3-24x^2}{8x^2}$

$8x^2(-2x-3)$

# Leading Coefficient
* the number in front of the variable term with the highest exponent is the leading coefficient
* when factoring you want to leave the resulting polynomial with a *positive* leading coefficient

# Example
## a)
$\frac{3 - x}{-1}$

$-1(-3+x)$
or 
$-1 (x-3)$
## b)
$-20x^3 - 45x$
GCF: $-5x$
$\frac{-20x^3 - 45x}{-5x}$
$-5x(4x^2+9)$
(no difference of squares, all done)
## c)
$18x-27x^3$

GCF: $-9x$

$\frac{18x-27x^3}{-9x}$

$-9x(-2+3x^2)$
or
$-9x(3x^2-2)$


Factor numerator and denominator completely (positiveleading coefficient). 
• State non-permissible values as restrictions BEFORE simplifying. 
• Simplify by removing common factors (not common terms). Divide out the common factors in the numerator and denominator.

# Example 1

a)
$\frac{14x^5}{3x^2}$
NPV: x $\ne$ 0
simplified: $\frac{7x^3}{2}$

b)
$\frac{-24x^2}{18x^3}$
cant factor anything

NPV: $x \ne 0$

simplify: $\frac{-4x^{-1}}{3}\to\frac{-4}{3x}$

c)$\frac{15x^3-5x}{15x^3}$
factor: $5x\left( \frac{3x^2-1}{15x^3} \right)$
NPV: $x\ne 0$
simplify: $\frac{1(3x^2 - 1)}{3x^2}\to\frac{3x^2}{3x^2}$

d)$\frac{3x-9x^2}{6x^3}$
factor: $\frac{3x(1-3x)}{6x^3}$
NPV: $x\ne{0}$
simplify: $\frac{1-3x}{2x^2}$

e) $\frac{6x^2-8x}{3x^3-3x^2}$
factor: $\frac{2x(3x-4)}{x^2(3x-4)}$
NPV: $x\ne 0,\frac{4}{3}$
simplify: $\frac{2}{x}$

f)$\frac{2x-1}{4x^2-1}$
factor: $\frac{2x-1}{(2x+1)(2x-1)}$ 
NPV: $x\ne \pm \frac{1}{2}$
$\frac{1}{2x+1}$

$\frac{1-x}{x^2-1}$
factor: $\frac{1-x}{(x+1)(x-1)}$
$\frac{1-x}{(x-1)(x+1)}$
NPV: $x\ne \pm 1$
$\frac{-1}{x+1}$

h) $\frac{2c-8}{16-4c}$
factor: $\frac{2(c-4)}{4(4-c)}$
NPV: $x\ne 4$
$\frac{1(-1)}{2}$ $\to$ $\frac{-1}{2}$

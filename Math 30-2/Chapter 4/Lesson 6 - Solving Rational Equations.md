
# Vocabulary 
Rational equation - An equation that involves one or more rational expressions

Now, we will work with equations. The difference between and equation and an expression is simply an equal
# Solving rational equations
* factor completely (if possible) and state NPV's
* Determine the common denominator, and
* multiply all terms in the equation by the common denominator
* the equation will result in a linear or quadratic equation that needs to be solved IF the equation is not linear or quadratic look for a GCF that can be factored
	* LINEAR: Variable on one side of the equation and number on the other
	* QUADRATIC: Use inverse operations to get the entire equation on one side and zero on the other $ax^2 +bx+c=0$. Use the quadratic formulator factoring to solve for x
		* $x=\frac{-b\pm \sqrt{ b^2-4ac }}{2a}$ (on formula sheet)

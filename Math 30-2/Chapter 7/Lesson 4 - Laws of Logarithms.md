
# Laws of logarithms (all on formula sheet):
## Multiplication Law: 
### $\log_{a}(bc)=\log_{a}(b)+\log_{a}(c)$
## division Law:
### $\log _{a}\left( \frac{b}{c} \right)=\log_{a}(b)-\log_{a}(c)$
## Power Law: 
### $\log_{a}(b^c) =c\log_{a}(b)$

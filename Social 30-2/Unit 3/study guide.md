Key Principles of Communist Ideology

- Collectivism - people work for the group - the state provides the necessities of life
    
- Revolution
    
- The dictatorship of the Proletariat
    
- Egalitarianism
    
- Public Enterprise
    

  

Communism in the USSR

- Lenin took Marx's version of scientific socialism to fit a Russian model and therefore is different than Marxism as Marxism was only theory, not practice
    
- After Lenin and the Bolsheviks won the civil war, they quickly tried to implement the communist ideology in an idea known as "war communism"
    
- War communism didn't work out as hoped and the NEP (New Economic Policy) was put in place to relieve the pressure
    
- Stalin instilled 5-year plans in order to collectivize and modernize the Soviet economy
    
- Kulaks who didn't give up their land during collectivization were jailed and/or sent to the gulags
    
- Any perceived opposition of Stalin was arrested and executed, including a period called "The Great Purge" which got rid of potential rivals within his own party.
    
- Propaganda was a key tool in control over the citizens
    

  

Key Principles of Fascism

- Militarism
    
- Imperialism
    
- Traditionalism
    
- Nationalism
    
- Cult of leadership - total allegiance to the leader
    

  

Key Principles of Extreme Fascism

- Racism
    
- Extreme Nationalism (Ultranationalism)
    
- Big-business-state-military partnerships
    

  
  

Fascism in Nazi Germany

- The Nazi's were different than the Italians in their incorporation of anti-semitism as part of their fascist ideology creating a clear divide in their nationalism, moving it from strong nationalism to ultranationalism.
    
- Citizens worked for the benefit of the state and therefore anti-liberalism was promoted (anti-individualism)
    
- Hitler was elected to power but created a crisis that gave him emergency powers to take complete control over the nation as a dictatorship.  This crisis was an excuse to restrict rights and freedoms and ban all political parties except the Nazi Party
    
- Hitler purged other potential leaders within his own party in the "Night of the Long Knives"
    
- Persecution of Jews started in 1933 and the period before the war included being barred for working for the government, the inability to marry Aryan Germans, not allowed to hold/touch the flag of Germany, not allowed to change their names, closing of their businesses and children expelled from schools.
    
- Courtship of the young occurred in order to brainwash them in the Nazi ideology.
    
- Hitler received much of his financial support from major industrialists due to being anti-communist and his anti-union stance.

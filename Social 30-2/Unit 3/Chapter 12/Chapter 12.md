## 11. Define:

### Fiscal Policy
* The use of government spending and tax revenue to influence the economy
* Change in government spending and tax rates
* set by the government 
* no specific target (indirectly unemployment rate)
* side effect on government budget/borrowing
* strong political dimension to changing tax rates

### Monetary Policy
* Use of a central bank to control the amount of money supply
* Change in interest raets / money supply
* set by a central bank
* target inflation
* side effect on exchange rate and housing market
* mostly independent from the political process

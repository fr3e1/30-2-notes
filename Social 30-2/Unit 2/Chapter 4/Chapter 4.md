# Give the definition of mercantilism and provide a couple of the main points

[[Mercantilism]]: an economic system, common in Europe from the 1500s to the 1700s, based on the [[beliefs]] that a country should export more goods than it imports, accumulate wealth in gold and silver, and protect its economy by taxing setting quotas and imports. In this system, a government exercised much control over the economy of its country, through corporations, trading company monopolies, and regulation of production

main points: 
* max exports, min imports
* government exercised lots of control in the economy

## 5. List below a number of the positives and negatives that were created by classical liberalism and the industrial revolution
## positives
* promoted individual rights and freedoms
* encouraged free markets and competition
* reduced power of monarchs/church, moving towards democracy
* huge boost in production, trade, and technology
* created jobs and raised overall standard of living overtime
## negatives
* focused on individual wealth, often ignoring social inequality
* weak government role meant poor working conditions and no labor protections
* favored the wealthy, leaving the poor with little voice
* harsh factory conditions (child labor, long hours, low pay)
* pollution and environmental damage increased 
* widened the gap between rich and poor
* overcrowded, unhealthy cities with poor sanitation

# 6. How would you describe disparity (gap between the rich and poor) at the turn of the century in wages, living conditions, working conditions?

## wages
rich: industrialists and business owners made massive fortunes
poor: factory and mine workers earned very low pay, barely enough for food and rent. Many families relied on child labor to survive

## living conditions
rich: lived in masions or large suburban homes with comfort, servants, and access to new technology (electricity, telephone)

poor: crowded into tenments/slums in industrial cities. Overcrowding, no proper sanitation, and disease were common

## working conditions
rich: controlled businesses, enjoyed wealth without physical risk

poor: faced unsafe factories, long hours, dangerous machinery, and no safety protections. Injury or illness often meant losing their job

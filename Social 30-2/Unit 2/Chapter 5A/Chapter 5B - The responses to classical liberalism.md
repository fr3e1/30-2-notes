## 1. Who were luddites
A group of people that believed that industrialization was NOT progressive

between 1812-1816, luddites broke in to factories, destroyed machines, and attacked factory owners in their homes

## 2. What were the Factory Acts of Britain
series of acts passed by the parliament of the UK to regulate and improve the conditions of industrial employment 

## 3a. What were some restrictions the Factory Acts put in place?
regulated hours of work and moral welfare f young children
